# Privacy Policy for InvoiceSlayer

*Last Updated: February 18, 2026*

InvoiceSlayer ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Data We Collect
We collect and process the following categories of personal data:
- **Client Information**: Name, email address, physical address, phone number, and VAT/tax identifiers.
- **Financial Information**: Invoices, quotes, and payment transaction history.
- **Project Data**: Project names, descriptions, and associated expenses.
- **Usage Data**: Technical information such as IP addresses (stored in audit logs).

## 2. Purpose of Processing
We process your data to:
- Provide invoicing and quote management services.
- Process payments via third-party providers (Stripe).
- Send automated reminders and recurring invoices.
- Maintain an audit log for security and compliance (SOC2).

## 3. Data Retention
- **Personal Data**: Retained as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for a period of 90 days.
- **Legal Obligations**: We may retain certain data for longer periods if required by law (e.g., for tax purposes).

## 4. Your Rights (GDPR)
Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access (Art. 15)**: You can request a copy of your personal data.
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17)**: You can request that we delete your personal data.
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)**: You can request an export of your data in a structured format.

You can exercise these rights via the **Privacy Settings** page in the application or by using our GDPR API endpoints.

## 5. Security
We implement robust security measures including:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.
- Modern security headers (CSP, HSTS).
- API authentication and rate limiting.
- Regular audit logging of all data operations.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact the administrator of this InvoiceSlayer instance.
